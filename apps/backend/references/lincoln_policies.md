# Lincoln Financial individual life insurance policies

Research reviewed: **October 4, 2026**. Starting point: [Lincoln's life insurance page][life].

This catalog describes the individual products listed on Lincoln's public life insurance, IUL, VUL, and MoneyGuard pages at review time. It includes survivorship coverage and state-specific MoneyGuard offerings. Employer group life/AD&D, institutional/private-placement products, and a complete discontinued-policy inventory are outside this catalog. A listing is evidence of public marketing, not confirmation that a particular version is accepting applications in every state or distribution channel. [Product portfolio][portfolio], [IUL lineup][iul], [VUL lineup][vul], [MoneyGuard solutions][moneyguard].

Use this as an educational reference. The issued contract, state-approved riders, underwriting decision, and current illustration determine actual coverage. Premium quotes are individualized; this document does not estimate prices or recommend a product.

## How to read the coverage options

- **Term** provides temporary death-benefit protection without cash-value accumulation. Level premiums apply for the selected period; continuation afterward can change both premiums and coverage. [Term overview][term].
- **IUL** credits interest using an index formula, rather than investing directly in the index. Caps, participation rates, and floors affect credited interest. Policy charges can reduce value even when an index floor prevents a negative index credit. [WealthBuilder launch][builder-launch].
- **VUL** lets the owner choose investment allocations. Account values can fall, including loss of principal. The policy also has insurance and investment expenses. [VUL overview][vul].
- **Survivorship** insures two people and generally pays the base death benefit after the second death. A first-death payment or living benefit requires a specific applicable option/rider. [WealthPreserve guide][preserve-sheet], [AssetEdge SVUL launch][asset-launch].
- **Permanent coverage** is potential lifelong protection, not an unconditional promise. Premiums must support charges or satisfy a no-lapse guarantee. A guarantee has its own funding and transaction conditions; its duration can be shorter than the policy's potential lifetime. [Lincoln 2025 annual report, Life Insurance section][annual-report].

For the three-option permanent products below, **Option 1** is a level specified amount; **Option 2** adds account value; **Option 3** adds cumulative premiums, with contract-specific adjustments for withdrawals. These options do not bypass loans, accelerated benefits, or other reductions. Verify the precise calculation in the applicable fact sheet and contract.

## Term life

### Lincoln TermAccel Level Term

- **Coverage:** $100,000–$2.5 million; level premium periods of 10, 15, 20, or 30 years. No cash value.
- **Issue ages:** 18–60 for 10/15/20-year terms; 30-year maximum is 55 for nontobacco and 50 for tobacco classes.
- **Application:** Electronic process; qualified applicants may avoid lab work. Approval and lab waivers are not guaranteed.
- **Conversion:** To qualifying Lincoln permanent policies before the level period ends or attained age 70, whichever comes first.
- **Continuation:** A one-time face-amount reduction occurs after the level period. Subsequent premium patterns vary; Lincoln describes possible three-premium level intervals followed by annual increases. Ask for the actual renewal schedule through age 95.
- **Riders:** Terminal-illness acceleration, with a charge upon use; children's term coverage and disability waiver of premium cost extra. The acceleration described requires a terminal illness likely to result in death within six months.
- **Availability:** Not available in New York; riders vary by state.

Source: [TermAccel product details][termaccel].

### Lincoln LifeElements Level Term

- **Coverage:** $250,000 minimum; maximum subject to individual underwriting. Level premiums for 10, 15, 20, or 30 years; no cash value.
- **Issue ages outside NY:** 10-year: 18–80; 15-year: nontobacco 18–75/tobacco 18–70; 20-year: nontobacco 18–70/tobacco 18–65; 30-year: nontobacco 18–55/tobacco 18–50.
- **Application:** Online/telephone interview available. Qualified LincXpress applicants through age 60 and up to $2.5 million may avoid labs; this is not the overall coverage ceiling.
- **Conversion:** Without new underwriting to qualifying permanent policies before the level period ends or age 70, whichever comes first.
- **Continuation:** Face amount decreases once after the level period; premiums then increase annually if coverage continues, through age 95.
- **Riders:** Terminal-illness acceleration with a charge upon use; children's term and disability waiver of premium at extra cost.
- **Availability conflict:** The term overview says unavailable in NY, but this product page publishes NY ages and a contractual-obligations restriction for its 10-year term. Confirm NY availability directly rather than interpreting the table as a current offer.

Sources: [LifeElements product details][lifeelements], [term overview][term].

## Indexed universal life

### Lincoln WealthProtector IUL

- **Coverage/ages:** $100,000 minimum; maximum underwritten. Preferred classes: 20–80; standard nontobacco: 0–80; standard tobacco: 15–80, using age nearest birthday.
- **Funding/duration:** Flexible premiums; 15-year base no-lapse protection requires sufficient premiums. Potential continuation to age 121 depends on funding; age 121 changes premium and deduction rules.
- **Death benefit:** Options 1, 2, and 3. Extended No-Lapse Rider II requires Option 1 and election at issue. Premium timing, loans, withdrawals, and benefit changes affect its duration.
- **Cash value:** Traditional and volatility-controlled indexed accounts, plus a fixed account. Growth is not guaranteed.
- **Riders:** Terminal and chronic illness acceleration have no upfront rider cost, but benefit-payment costs/reductions may apply. Children's term and disability deduction waiver cost extra. Multiplier must be elected at issue and costs extra when active. Enhanced overloan protection has eligibility conditions.

Source: [WealthProtector fact sheet, pp. 1–2 and 7][protector-sheet]. Lincoln's [February 2026 launch notice][protector-launch] describes an optional guarantee customizable up to age 100 and states the product is unavailable in NY or CA; confirm subsequent state approvals before purchase.

### Lincoln WealthAccelerate IUL

- **Coverage/ages:** Published range $100,000–$1.5 million; ages 20–55, age nearest birthday.
- **Funding/duration:** Flexible premiums; 10-year no-lapse guarantee subject to required funding. Lifelong potential requires adequate value/premiums after that period; premium/deduction rules change at age 121.
- **Death benefit:** Option 1 (level) or Option 2 (specified amount plus account value).
- **Cash value:** S&P 500 and Fidelity-linked indexed accounts with fixed-account access; loans and withdrawals reduce benefits and may cause lapse.
- **Riders:** Published options include critical-illness acceleration, LifeAssure chronic-illness acceleration, and overloan protection. Benefit fees, discounts, eligibility, and exercise charges vary.
- **Application/availability:** Streamlined electronic underwriting and lab-free consideration for qualifying applicants; not guaranteed acceptance. Not available in NY. Older professional material also restricts Massachusetts use; obtain current state-specific confirmation.

Sources: [Lincoln-authored client fact sheet, hosted by The Cason Group, pp. 1–4][accelerate-sheet], [2023 launch and eligibility details][accelerate-launch], [Lincoln underwriting guide][accelerate-underwriting]. The client sheet is an older version; do not treat its quoted index rates as current.

### Lincoln WealthBuilder IUL

- **Coverage/ages:** $100,000 minimum ($25,000 for eligible Guaranteed Issue cases); maximum underwritten. Preferred: 20–80; standard nontobacco: 0–80/tobacco: 15–80; Simplified/Guaranteed Issue: 20–70. These programs have separate eligibility requirements.
- **Funding/duration:** Flexible premiums; 10-year conditional no-lapse guarantee and potential continuation to age 121. Sufficient value or additional premiums are needed afterward.
- **Death benefit:** Options 1, 2, and 3.
- **Cash value:** S&P/Fidelity-linked accounts, including volatility-controlled choices, and a fixed account. Optional multiplier has a charge when active and must be selected at issue.
- **Riders:** Chronic/terminal illness and LTC acceleration choices, LifeAssure ABR II, children's term, disability deduction waiver, enhanced overloan protection, Change of Insured, and business surrender-value enhancements. Extra-cost and funding conditions differ by rider.
- **Costs/availability:** Premium loads, insurance and administrative charges, and surrender charges apply; request a current illustration. Not available in NY.

Source: [WealthBuilder fact sheet, pp. 1–2 and 6–8][builder-sheet]. The [launch announcement][builder-launch] explains its accumulation focus and warns that index protection does not prevent policy charges from reducing value.

### Lincoln WealthBuilder ECV IUL

- **Coverage/ages:** $100,000 minimum; maximum underwritten. Ages 20–70, nearest birthday; 71–75 may receive individual consideration.
- **Funding/duration:** Flexible premiums; 10-year conditional no-lapse guarantee, with potential continuation to age 121 if adequately funded.
- **Death benefit:** Options 1, 2, and 3; indexed and fixed-account cash-value choices.
- **Early cash value:** Base design reduces early surrender charges. ECVE Options 1/2 remove policy surrender charges during the initial period while changing other charges; they do not eliminate all costs or guarantee growth.
- **Riders:** LifeAssure ABR II, enhanced overloan protection, multiplier, and Change of Insured; multiplier election is at issue and costs extra when active.
- **Availability:** Not available in NY; state-specific rider approval applies.

Source: [ECV fact sheet, pp. 1–2 and 6–8][ecv-sheet]. Lincoln's [May 2025 company-issued announcement][ecv-launch] explains the business/early-liquidity purpose. Premium financing remains a separate loan arrangement; Lincoln does not offer or endorse it, and financing creates interest-rate, collateral, and lapse risks.

### Lincoln WealthPreserve 2 SIUL

- **Coverage/ages:** Two lives, with the base benefit payable after the second death. The guide lists $100,000 minimum ($25,000 for eligible Guaranteed Issue); maximum underwritten. Preferred nontobacco ages 20–80; standard nontobacco/tobacco 20–85, nearest birthday.
- **Funding/duration:** Flexible premiums; 25-year base no-lapse guarantee subject to funding. Extended No-Lapse Minimum Premium Rider requires Option 1 and extends protection based on the younger insured's age. Obtain the exact guaranteed endpoint in the illustration; potential continuation to the younger insured's age 121 is separate.
- **Death benefit/cash value:** Options 1/2/3; S&P/Fidelity-linked indexed and fixed accounts.
- **Riders/options:** Overloan protection, estate protection, and a conditional policy-split option. Accelerated-benefit variants with/without a first-death benefit and Supplemental Increase Rider are listed; confirm their actual trigger, charge, and availability.
- **Availability:** Not available in NY. This is SIUL, not the similarly named single-life WealthPreserve 2 IUL.

Source: [WealthPreserve 2 SIUL client guide/fact sheet, pp. 7 and 10–12][preserve-sheet].

## Variable universal life

### Lincoln AssetEdge VUL (2025)

- **Coverage/ages:** $100,000 minimum; Simplified Issue $50,000/Guaranteed Issue $25,000, subject to program eligibility. Underwritten maximum; $5 million maximum for those two programs. Preferred ages 20–80; standard nontobacco 0–80/tobacco 15–80; special programs 20–70, nearest birthday.
- **Funding/duration:** Flexible premiums and potential coverage to age 121. Base no-lapse provision is up to 20 years, dependent on issue age and required premiums; it is not lifetime protection.
- **Death benefit:** Options 1/2/3.
- **Cash value/riders:** More than 75 investment choices, four indexed accounts, fixed account, and model portfolios. Living-benefit/LTC, disability-waiver, and surrender-value riders have conditions and possible costs. Enhanced Allocation Rider has an ongoing charge when elected.
- **Availability:** Not available in NY; investment losses and policy charges can require additional funding.

Sources: [2025 fact sheet][asset2025-sheet], [consumer product page][asset2025-page]. The [2025 launch notice][asset-launch] identifies enhanced overloan protection for eligible heavily loaned policies and removes the earlier indexed-account allocation threshold. Neither feature guarantees investment returns.

### Lincoln AssetEdge VUL (2022)

- **Status:** Still listed on the public VUL page, although the main portfolio promotes 2025. Confirm acceptance of new 2022 applications by state/channel.
- **Coverage/ages:** Same published coverage thresholds and underwriting-class age ranges as the 2025 entry, per its own fact sheet; do not infer other features are identical.
- **Funding/duration:** Flexible premiums; maturity age 121. No-lapse protection up to 20 years, grading down by issue age to 10 years at ages 60+, subject to required premiums.
- **Death benefit:** Options 1/2/3.
- **Cash value/riders:** More than 75 variable investments, four indexed accounts, and fixed account. Optional chronic/LTC acceleration, critical-illness benefit, disability deduction waiver, Change of Insured, and surrender-value enhancement. Overloan Protection Rider is included for eligible GPT policies, charges upon exercise, and is unavailable with CVAT.
- **Availability:** Not available in NY. Loans, withdrawals, investment losses, and charges can reduce value and jeopardize coverage.

Sources: [2022 fact sheet][asset2022-sheet], [2022 consumer page][asset2022-page], [VUL lineup][vul]. GPT/CVAT are alternative tax qualification tests; request the contract-specific restrictions from the advisor.

### Lincoln AssetEdge SVUL

- **Coverage/ages:** Two insureds; base payout after the second death. $250,000 minimum; maximum underwritten. Both insureds ages 20–80, nearest birthday.
- **Funding/duration:** Flexible premiums; maturity at younger insured's age 121. Conditional base no-lapse period is the lesser of 20 years or younger insured's age 80; a minimum 10-year duration applies when the younger insured's issue age exceeds 70.
- **Death benefit:** Options 1/2/3.
- **Cash value/riders:** More than 75 investments, four indexed accounts, fixed account, model portfolios; optional Enhanced Allocation and estate-protection riders. Eligible GPT policies have enhanced overloan protection conditions.
- **Living benefits:** Survivorship LifeAssure ABR and the alternative terminal-illness ABR can only be exercised after the first death. No upfront charge does not mean free acceleration: discounts and administrative fees apply.
- **Availability:** Not available in NY. Investment value can fall; insurance, investment, and surrender charges apply.

Sources: [SVUL fact sheet, pp. 1 and 5–8][svul-sheet], [Lincoln launch notice][asset-launch].

## MoneyGuard life insurance with long-term-care benefits

MoneyGuard combines life insurance with LTC benefits rather than providing standalone traditional LTC insurance. LTC payments draw down policy benefits; unused benefits may leave a death benefit. Coverage does not promise to pay every care expense. [MoneyGuard overview][moneyguard].

### MoneyGuard Fixed Advantage (2025)

- **Coverage/ages:** One life; $50,000–$500,000 specified death benefit; ages 40–80, last birthday.
- **Funding/guarantees:** Single payment or scheduled payments up to 10 years, shorter at some ages. Benefits depend on paying scheduled premiums fully/on time without loans, withdrawals, or surrenders. Value Protection Endorsement supplies conditional no-lapse protection.
- **LTC options:** Published benefit durations 3–6 years. At first claim, irrevocably choose reimbursement or indemnity; indemnity pays 80% of the monthly maximum without reducing the total benefit pool. Optional 3%/5% compound inflation costs extra. No elimination period once eligible.
- **Care:** Home health, adult day care, assisted living, nursing home, hospice, respite, and specified ancillary services, subject to definitions/limits.
- **Other benefits:** Included Benefit Transfer Rider requires both parties to hold eligible policies. Included terminal-illness acceleration terminates the LTC rider when used. Return-of-premium choices: basic 70%, or extra-cost vested option reaching 100% in year 11, subject to contract adjustments.

Sources: [2025 fact sheet, pp. 1–4][fixed-sheet], [consumer product page][fixed-page]. LTC claims reduce remaining life benefits; residual death benefits follow policy specifications. Lincoln's current fact sheet excludes NY; older materials also exclude CA. Confirm the currently approved state/form rather than assuming every state receives this 2025 design.

### MoneyGuard Market Advantage (2024)

- **Coverage/ages:** One life; $50,000–$500,000 specified amount at issue; ages 30–70, last birthday. Single or flexible premiums, subject to limits and possible underwriting.
- **LTC benefits:** Base, market-driven, and protected reference values determine available benefits. Growth is not guaranteed. Monthly caps and total benefit limits are contract-specific; a fixed number of benefit years is not specified in the reviewed 2024 fact sheet.
- **Payment/care:** Irrevocable first-claim choice of reimbursement or indemnity; indemnity pays 80% of the monthly maximum. No elimination period once eligible. Covered settings include home health, adult day care, assisted living, nursing home, hospice, and respite care, with ancillary-service limits.
- **Guarantees/riders:** Included Value Protection Rider requires quarterly rebalancing and compliant allocations. Included Benefit Transfer Rider has eligibility conditions. Published surrender-charge period: 14 years.

Source: [2024 fact sheet, pp. 1–3][market-sheet]. The [May 2026 prospectus][market-prospectus] describes optional annual 5% compound inflation increases; declining one ends future purchase rights. LTC payments reduce specified amount and policy values. Market growth does not replace premium/guarantee requirements.

**State/version distinction:** The [general consumer page][market-page] excludes NY and CA; a separate [California page][market-ca-page] excludes NY and describes CA coverage. Use the state-approved contract. Do not transplant the 2024 design, indemnity choice, or benefit formulas into an older/CA policy without checking its form.

### MoneyGuard II (California listing)

- **Coverage:** One-life universal life with riders reimbursing qualified LTC expenses and remaining death-benefit protection. The reviewed consumer page does not specify issue ages, face-amount limits, or benefit-duration choices; request a current CA illustration.
- **Funding/guarantees:** Single payment or pay over time. Guaranteed benefits require all scheduled premiums on time/in full and no loans, withdrawals, or surrenders; the issuer backs the guarantee.
- **LTC options:** Optional 3% or 5% compound inflation at extra cost; no elimination period once eligible. International benefits are restricted to nursing/residential facilities.
- **Eligibility:** Licensed practitioner must certify qualifying chronic illness: inability to perform at least two activities of daily living for at least 90 days, or severe cognitive impairment requiring substantial supervision. Annual recertification applies.
- **Riders:** Value Protection, LTC Acceleration of Benefits, and optional LTC Extension of Benefits. Medical questions remain required despite no exam/labs.
- **Availability:** Consumer page says California only; its disclosure identifier is dated 2020. Confirm current new-business status rather than treating that older listing as proof of an active offer.

Source: [MoneyGuard II consumer page][moneyguard-ii].

## Costs, limitations, and items to confirm

- **Lapse and cash access:** Permanent-policy insurance/administrative charges continue even when growth disappoints. Loans accrue interest; loans/withdrawals can reduce death benefits and shorten guarantees. Ask for guaranteed and nonguaranteed illustrations, surrender-charge schedules, and premiums needed after a guarantee ends. [Annual report, Life Insurance section][annual-report].
- **VUL expenses:** Insurance, premium-load, administrative, mortality/expense, and investment-management charges may apply. Read the current policy and fund prospectuses; index-account protection does not protect all variable investments. [VUL overview][vul], [prospectus directory][prospectuses].
- **Tax wording:** Death benefits and qualifying LTC benefits are generally income-tax-free subject to applicable rules. Cash distributions have additional conditions; MEC policies, surrender, or lapse with loans can create tax consequences. MoneyGuard is commonly a MEC. Obtain individualized tax guidance before relying on a distribution strategy. [MoneyGuard Market Advantage disclosures][market-page], [2026 prospectus, Tax Issues][market-prospectus].
- **Living benefits:** Acceleration uses some of the death benefit; fees/discounts, qualifying diagnoses, exclusions, and public-assistance consequences can apply. A chronic-illness rider is not automatically equivalent to an LTC expense rider. Compare the actual trigger and payment terms. [AssetEdge 2022 rider explanations][asset2022-page], [MoneyGuard II eligibility][moneyguard-ii].
- **Before an application:** Confirm state, exact version/policy form, insured ages, underwriting class, desired death-benefit option, premium schedule, guarantee endpoint, riders and charges. For MoneyGuard, confirm monthly/total LTC limits, benefit duration, inflation election, care-provider definitions, indemnity availability, residual death benefit, and claim exclusions.
