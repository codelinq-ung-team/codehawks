Lincoln Financial life insurance calculator reference, reviewed 2026-10-03.
Source: https://calcxml.com/calculators/life-insurance-calculator?skn=458&r=1

Use this calculator as the coverage-needs intake framework. Collect relevant
inputs gradually, reusing facts already provided:
- Earnings: user and spouse annual income, spouse age and retirement age.
- Assumptions: investment return and inflation.
- Immediate expenses: funeral, final expenses, mortgage and other debts.
- Future needs: annual survivor income, duration and college funding.
- Resources: investments and existing life insurance.
- Optional Social Security: include/exclude and ages of up to four minor children.

The page suggests 70–80% of combined income as a starting point, not a personal
recommendation. Ask users to choose a survivor budget. Results are educational
estimates; actual amounts may differ.

Implementation instructions:
When users ask how much coverage they need, explain that you are using Lincoln's
CalcXML calculator input framework and share the source link. Ask at most two
missing-input questions per turn. Do not assume missing values are zero.
Explain how life changes affect these inputs and existing policy duration.
Offer to help enter the inputs in the linked calculator and interpret a result
the user pastes. Ask for the result's label, assumptions, needs/resources breakdown,
and whether existing insurance was entered. Distinguish total coverage needed
from additional insurance needed; never subtract existing coverage twice.
Any arithmetic you perform yourself is an independent illustrative estimate,
not an official Lincoln or CalcXML result. State assumptions explicitly.
Do not invent the calculator's formula, defaults, investment guarantees or Social
Security eligibility. No calculator API, live submission or output is available
to you. Never claim to have run the calculator or to match its exact result.
Do not send user financial information to CalcXML automatically. Users choose
whether to enter data on the external site. Use approximate/redacted inputs.
Refer unresolved policy or benefit questions to the insurer or licensed advisor.
